How I Work

What I've Chosen to Master

Not a list of skills. These are the operating principles behind everything I create.

01
Storytelling +
Every idea goes through a story first.

Every idea I have goes through a story before it reaches anyone. Not because storytelling is a technique — but because it's the only format the human mind actually absorbs and retains. If I can't tell something as a story I don't understand it well enough yet. Facts inform. Stories transform. Everything I create is built around this.

Filter question:
Can I make someone feel this, not just understand it?
02
Meaningful Creation +
Value in someone's life is the only metric.

I can't sustain something I'm doing purely for money or status. What actually drives me is whether what I'm building matters to another person. Revenue and recognition are results — not the goal. If I'm creating content that doesn't genuinely help or move someone I'm producing noise. I refuse to produce noise.

Filter question:
Does this create real value in someone else's life?
03
Purpose +
No deeper why, no start. Simple as that.

I don't begin anything without knowing the why — not the surface why but the deep one. The kind that keeps you going when motivation disappears and results haven't arrived yet. Purpose is the engine underneath everything I build. Without it effort is just activity. With it even the hardest days have direction.

Filter question:
Would I still do this if nobody was watching?
04
Impact +
Everything aims at a real outcome for a real person.

Content for content's sake is noise. Consistency for consistency's sake is just showing up. Everything I create is aimed at a real outcome in a real person's life — a shift in thinking a decision made clearer a belief challenged. If I can't answer what changes for someone after consuming this I don't publish it.

Filter question:
Did this actually move something for someone?
05
Authenticity +
If it's not genuinely mine I won't sustain it — and without sustainability there's no long game.

The things I keep doing long-term are always the things that feel genuinely mine. Inauthenticity compounds — it becomes harder to maintain quality drops and eventually I abandon it. Authenticity is not a moral position. It's a survival strategy for anyone building something over years. Honest to myself first. Honest to the world second.

Filter question:
Can I look at this a year from now and feel it was truly mine?
